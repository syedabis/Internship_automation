QUESTIONS

Before you request a portal

Do we need to migrate our existing spreadsheet?+

No. Certify reads and writes the workshop data you already have — there's no export or import step to get started.

What if a participant's university isn't in the list?+

Let us know and we'll add it. The list is maintained centrally, so every portal benefits from the addition, not just yours.

Can more than one person on our team have access?+

Yes — invite as many teammates as you need, each with their own login and role. Admins manage codes and templates; Viewers track status.

How long does it take to get a portal set up?+

A few minutes. Pick a workshop, upload your certificate design, and you're ready to start collecting submissions.

Is our data visible to other societies on the platform?+

No. Every portal is scoped to its own workshop on the server — not just hidden in the interface — so one society's register is never reachable from another's account.
